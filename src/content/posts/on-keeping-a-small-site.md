A lot of personal sites get dressed up as brands before they have anything to
say. A custom domain can wait. What matters first is a page you will actually
open, a place to put a sentence before it evaporates.

This one is intentionally small. Two or three notes, a short about page, type
that is comfortable to read. If it stays that way, that is fine. If it grows,
it can grow from here — still at blog.haily.yevklnekqt.

## On length

There is no need for a prospectus. A site can be a few pages in Computer
Modern, with an abstract and a table of contents, and still be finished for
the afternoon.
